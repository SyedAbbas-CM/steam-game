# Aria Region — Creative Design

## What We're Working With

```
                         WILD1 (50x50)
                     thieves, wild dogs
                   "Endless Caverns" dungeon
                    /        |        \
              FOR1          |         VLY1
                          CAMP (20x20)
                        Ryu the trainer
                            |
                         ARIA (25x25)
                    Arnie's Weapons (23 items)
                    Crooked Beak Inn (Yoregot)
                    Shrowded Orb Magic (Morin)
                    Town Hall (Elder)
                    Guard blocks north exit < Lvl 6
                    Jimbob (wanderer), Old Man, Hawkeye
                   /                          \
            GRAVEYARD (20x30)              FARM (25x25)
            skeletons (20% spawn)       sheep, cows (passive)
            graves + trees              Dafydd (farmer)
            Aria-Crypt (3 floors)       Blaidd (maps), Mabion (pets)
                                        Plentyn (kid)
                                        Farm-base dungeon (6 floors)
```

**Aria town itself:** 4 buildings arranged around open ground. Chickens wander everywhere. A guard blocks the north gate until you're level 6. The south road leads to a graveyard. The east road leads to a farm. It's small, cozy, and boring — which is perfect for a starting town that the player will eventually outgrow.

**The problem with LoY's quests:** They're disconnected errands. "Kill 20 skeletons" doesn't tell you WHY skeletons are in the graveyard. "Kill fire beasts at the farm" doesn't explain why fire beasts are attacking a sheep farm. There's no thread connecting them. For our game, these quests need to become a **story** that also teaches the kingdom-building mechanics.

---

## The Aria Story

### The Setup

You arrive in Aria as a nobody. The town is small but peaceful. People are friendly. But something's wrong — the Elder seems worried, the Guard won't let anyone leave, and the graveyard south of town has been... active lately.

The underlying story: **Aria is dying.** The Guard blocks the north exit because the wilderness beyond is too dangerous — but also because if people leave, the town empties. The farm to the east is struggling. The graveyard's dead are waking up. Aria was once part of a larger kingdom that collapsed, and now it's a forgotten backwater with maybe 30 people in it. The Elder remembers better days but doesn't know how to fix things.

Your hero is the catalyst. Not because you're chosen or special — but because you're willing to do the dirty work nobody else will.

### Quest Chain: "The Bones Beneath" (Reworked q1)

**Original:** Kill 20 skeletons. Get 500 gold.

**New version:**

The Elder pulls you aside after you've walked around town. "Traveler — I wouldn't normally ask a stranger, but we have no one else. The graveyard south of town... the dead aren't staying dead. My people won't go near it, and we can't bury our dead. Old Maelor died last week and his family's keeping the body in their cellar."

You go to the graveyard. It's atmospheric — gravestones everywhere, trees closing in, and skeletons wandering between the plots. Not a swarm, just... wrong. A 20% spawn rate means you encounter them sporadically, which is creepier than a constant onslaught.

**The twist:** When you've killed enough skeletons and explored deeper, you find the entrance to the Aria-Crypt. Inside (3 floors), the source: a cracked burial vault where something is leaking dark energy. You can't fix it yet — you're too weak — but you report back.

The Elder is grateful but troubled. "The crypt was sealed generations ago. If it's broken open... we need to find out why. But first — can you check on Dafydd? He hasn't sent supplies in days."

**Reward:** 500 gold + the Elder trusts you now (unlocks next quests). The skeletons keep spawning (the crypt is still broken) but at a reduced rate.

**What this teaches:** Combat basics, the graveyard zone, dungeon exploration, and it sets up a mystery.

### Quest Chain: "The Farm" (Reworked a4 + New Content)

**Original a4:** Kill fire beasts at the farm. Collect 5 wings. Get a hat.

**New version:**

You walk east to Caws Mawr. Dafydd's farm is beautiful — sheep and cows grazing, crop fields, a cozy farmhouse. But Dafydd's panicked. "Fire beasts! Coming up from the cellar — the old storage pit we never use. They've already killed two sheep and burned the south field. Plentyn saw one near the house last night."

The kid Plentyn is terrified. Mabion the pet handler has locked his animals in the barn. Blaidd the map-maker is packing to leave.

You enter the Farm-base dungeon (6 floors). The deeper you go, the hotter it gets. The fire beasts are nesting in an old underground chamber — but WHY are they here? On the bottom floor, you find evidence: tunnels. Something dug up from below. Claw marks on the walls that are too big for fire beasts.

You clear the fire beasts (killing 5, collecting their wings as proof). Report back to Dafydd. He's relieved but you both know the tunnels mean something bigger is coming.

**Return to the Elder:** He examines the wings and the tunnel description. "Tunnels from below... the crypt breaking open... this isn't coincidence. Something is stirring underground. But we can't fight what we can't see. You need to get stronger. And we need help."

**Reward:** Head Dress + 500 gold + Dafydd offers to supply Aria with food (this is the seed of the town economy — Farm becomes your first "supply line"). The Elder suggests you visit the Training Camp.

**What this teaches:** The dungeon system, multi-floor exploration, and plants the idea that the zones are connected underground.

### Quest Chain: "Ryu's Trial" (New — Camp)

The Training Camp is a dead-end south of wild1. Just Ryu and an empty barracks. In LoY it's a nothing zone. In our game, it's where the kingdom-building tutorial happens.

Ryu is a retired soldier. He fought in the old kingdom's army before it fell. Now he runs a training camp that nobody comes to because there's no army to train for.

"You want to learn to fight? Good. But fighting alone won't save Aria. You need soldiers. And soldiers need a reason to fight."

**Ryu's Training (3 mini-tasks):**
1. Survive a wave of training dummies (combat tutorial if the player hasn't figured it out)
2. Clear the Camp-base dungeon (small, 2-3 floors — basically a training obstacle course)
3. Recruit your first militia — Ryu teaches you how. He becomes your first troop.

**Reward:** Ryu joins as a militia unit (your first troop). He also gives you a "Militia Banner" — the item that lets you recruit more troops at owned towns later. The Camp becomes a permanent training facility: any town you own can send recruits here for an upgrade (militia → trained soldier).

**What this teaches:** Troop recruitment, the garrison concept, and gives the player their first military unit.

### Quest Chain: "The North Gate" (Reworked Guard + q2)

**Original q2:** Deliver a package to Yoregot. Get 500 gold.

**New version — woven into the Guard storyline:**

The Guard at Aria's north gate is named in the script as just "Guard." Let's give him a name: **Aldric.** He's been standing at that gate for years, turning back anyone under level 6. He's not being cruel — he's seen what's out there.

After you've cleared the graveyard AND the farm, Aldric notices you. "You're different from the others. Most travelers just pass through. You actually helped." He doesn't move from the gate. "But the wilderness beyond... you'll need more than a sword."

**The delivery quest becomes meaningful:** The Elder asks you to bring a sealed letter (the "Package" from q2) to Yoregot at the Crooked Beak Inn. It's not a random errand — it's a **supply request.** Aria is running low on medicine and the Elder is asking Yoregot (who has trade contacts) to send word to other towns.

Yoregot reads the letter. "I'll send word. But it'll take time — and the roads aren't safe." He gives you a drink on the house and tells you about the wider world. "There used to be trade caravans running through the Wilderness every month. Merchants from Gomb, S'thel, even Toronega. Then the thieves moved in and the dogs went feral and the caravans stopped. If someone cleared even a path through..."

**You report back.** The Elder nods. "When you're ready, Aldric will let you through. But be careful — the Wilderness isn't like the graveyard. It's vast and dangerous."

**Reward:** 500 gold + 400 XP + Aldric now lets you pass regardless of level (quest completion overrides the level gate). This feels earned, not arbitrary.

**What this teaches:** That the world beyond Aria exists, that trade routes matter, and that NPCs have relationships with each other.

### Quest Chain: "Pelts and Proof" (Reworked q3)

**Original q3:** Collect 10 dog pelts from Valley. Get Pelt Cloak.

**New version:**

Once you can pass through wild1, the Elder mentions that Arnie the weaponsmith needs materials. "He can't make armor from nothing. The wild dogs in the forest and valley drop pelts — crude, but Arnie can work with them."

This quest takes you into wild1 for the first time. It's massive (50x50) and dangerous. Thieves and wild dogs everywhere. You're not going to the Valley (vly1) yet — the dogs in wild1 itself are enough.

**While hunting dogs,** you discover something: the thieves aren't random bandits. They're organized. They have a camp near the Endless Caverns entrance. This isn't a quest objective — it's just something you notice. It plants seeds for later content (who are these thieves? who's leading them? are they connected to whatever's stirring underground?).

You collect 10 pelts, bring them to Arnie. He's gruff but grateful. He makes you a Pelt Cloak and starts stocking better items (the quest-locked shop inventory opens up).

**Reward:** Pelt Cloak + 1000 XP + Arnie's shop improves. Also: you now know the wilderness layout, which prepares you for expansion.

## The Bigger Picture (What These Quests Build Toward)

After completing the Aria quest chain, the player has:

1. **Explored 5 zones** — Aria, Graveyard, Farm, Camp, Wild1
2. **Cleared 2 dungeons** — Aria-Crypt (partially), Farm-base
3. **Recruited 1 troop** — Ryu from Camp
4. **Established 1 supply line** — Farm → Aria food supply
5. **Discovered 2 mysteries** — What broke the crypt? Who leads the thieves?
6. **Unlocked the wilderness** — Free to explore in any direction

The next natural question is: "What do I do now?"

The answer comes from Yoregot's letter. Word gets back from other towns. **A trader from Gomb arrives** — first contact with the outside world. She tells you about the wider situation: every town is struggling. The old kingdom fell, trade stopped, monsters filled the gaps. Nobody has the strength to fix it alone.

The Elder approaches you with a proposition: **"You've done more for Aria in a few days than anyone's done in years. What if... what if you stayed? Not just as a traveler. As someone who helps us rebuild."**

This is the moment the player transitions from **wanderer** to **lord.** You're offered stewardship of Aria. You can build walls, recruit militia, establish trade. And the first thing you need to do is **secure your borders** — which means clearing the wilderness paths and eventually reaching the other towns.

## Zone Redesign Ideas

### Aria (25x25) — Keep Small, Add Character

The size is perfect — it should feel like a village you can walk across in 30 seconds. But it needs more life:

- **Add a town square** — a central open area where NPCs gather. Jimbob should wander through here. A market stall or two.
- **Add the Elder's house** — separate from Town Hall. He lives modestly. Bookshelves with lore.
- **Add a notice board** — near the town square. Shows available quests. Later, shows town status (food supply, garrison count, threat level).
- **Add a graveyard path** — the south exit should feel ominous. Cracked cobblestones, leaning fence posts. Visual storytelling.
- **The Crooked Beak should feel lived-in** — tables, mugs, a hearth. Hawkeye should be sitting in the corner drinking.

### Graveyard (20x30) — Make It Atmospheric

Right now it's graves + trees + skeletons. It should feel like a place with history:

- **Named graves** — a few readable headstones. "Here lies Captain Aldric's father" (connecting to the Guard). "The founders of Aria rest here." This gives the town history without an exposition dump.
- **The crypt entrance** — should be visually distinct. A stone archway half-buried in roots. The stairs going down should feel like descending into somewhere you shouldn't be.
- **Fog or particle effect** — if possible, a subtle mist. Green tint on the ground tiles near the crypt.
- **A memorial** — a statue or monument in the center. "To those who fell in the defense of Aria." Nobody alive remembers what they were defending against.

### Farm (25x25) — Make It Feel Productive

It's already charming with sheep and cows. Lean into the agricultural theme:

- **Crop fields** — the signs say "Spuds" and "Turnips." Make these visible tile areas that look like tilled earth. Later, farming could be a resource mechanic.
- **The storage pit** — currently just a sign. Make it the dungeon entrance with a broken trapdoor. Scorched earth around it (fire beasts came through here).
- **Animal pens** — fenced areas for the sheep and cows. When the farm is "owned," these produce food passively.
- **Dafydd's personality** — he's a simple farmer who loves his land. Not a quest-giver — a person. His dialogue should reflect the state of the farm (before clearing: panicked; after: grateful and optimistic).

### Wild1 (50x50) — The Real Challenge

This zone is critical because it's the gateway to everything. 50x50 is huge. It should feel overwhelming:

- **Distinct sub-areas** — the northwest should be rocky (toward mountains), the east should be forested (toward Hallows), the west more open (toward Valley), the center dense and dangerous.
- **The Endless Caverns** — should be a visible landmark. Rocky outcrop with a cave mouth. Signs of previous adventurers (abandoned campfire, broken weapons). This is the wild1 dungeon (5 floors) and it's harder than the Aria dungeons.
- **Thief camp** — not a quest objective yet, but visible. A cluster of tents near the east side. Smoke rising. More thieves spawn near it. This plants a seed: eventually you could raid the camp, clear the thieves, and make the wilderness safer (reducing spawn rates).
- **Crossroads markers** — stone cairns at the junctions where paths split to mountains/valley/forest/camp. Give the player a sense of direction in the huge zone.
- **The road to Aria** — a visible path (slightly lighter ground tiles) running south from the exit. This was the old trade road. It's overgrown but still visible. Restoring it could be a future quest (clearing trees, placing road tiles, making travel faster).

### Camp (20x20) — The Barracks

Currently empty and forgettable. It should feel military:

- **Training dummies** — wooden targets in the yard. Slash marks on them.
- **Weapon racks** — simple sprites along the barracks walls.
- **Ryu's quarters** — a small room with a cot, a sword on the wall, and a faded banner. The banner is from the old kingdom's army.
- **The water** — there's a pond/stream in the southeast. This could be where recruits train (a training ground visual).
- **Expansion potential** — this 20x20 zone could become a proper military base later. Building a barracks here, then a training ground, then walls. It's a dead-end zone which makes it defensible.

## The Underground Connection (Story Thread)

The crypt under the graveyard is broken. Tunnels were dug up under the farm. The Endless Caverns in the wilderness go deep. These are all connected — literally. Something underground is pushing creatures to the surface.

This isn't revealed all at once. The player pieces it together across multiple hero lives:

- **Hero 1:** Clears the graveyard, notices the crypt is cracked. Clears the farm, notices tunnels. Dies in the wilderness.
- **Hero 2:** Reaches the Endless Caverns, goes deeper. On floor 3, finds a collapsed passage that connects to the Aria-Crypt. Dies on floor 5.
- **Hero 3:** Makes it deeper. Finds ancient ruins — pre-Aria. The old kingdom built on top of something. Whatever it was, it's waking up.

This gives the roguelite death cycle narrative meaning. Each hero goes a little deeper. Each death reveals a little more. The kingdom above grows (towns, troops, trade) while the mystery below deepens.

## Summary: What the Player Experiences in the Aria Region

**First 30 minutes:**
Walk around Aria. Talk to people. Buy a dagger from Arnie. The Elder asks about skeletons. Go south to the graveyard. Fight your first enemies. Die or survive. Come back.

**First hour:**
Clear enough skeletons to satisfy the Elder. Discover the crypt. Go east to the farm. Meet Dafydd and his panicking family. Enter the farm dungeon. Fight fire beasts. Discover tunnels. Report back. The Elder is worried — the threats are connected.

**First evening:**
Deliver the letter. Talk to Yoregot about the world. Train with Ryu. Get your first troop. Aldric lets you through the north gate. Enter the Wilderness for the first time. It's massive and dangerous. Get pelts for Arnie. Spot the thief camp. Find the Endless Caverns. Maybe die. Maybe make it back.

**After several heroes:**
You own Aria. You've built a palisade. Ryu trains militia at the Camp. Dafydd's farm feeds the town. The graveyard is under control (not fixed, but manageable). You've cleared a path through the wilderness. The next step: reaching another town. Making an ally. Growing beyond this small corner of the world.

And always, the question: what's down there? What broke the crypt? What dug those tunnels? What lives at the bottom of the Endless Caverns?

You'll need a stronger hero to find out.
