# Setting — Ashenfold (40k-inspired)

> The quest structure stays the same. Beat-for-beat the same encounters, the
> same NPCs at the same locations, the same combat objectives. **Tone, names,
> and dialogue are reskinned** to a grimdark gothic-militarist universe.
>
> No Games Workshop IP is used. No Space Marines, no Emperor of Mankind, no
> Chaos Gods by name. Original world. 40k-shaped silhouette.

## One-paragraph premise

The First Cracking opened a wound in the world that has never closed. Sometimes the wound bleeds. The bleeding is called the Whisper. The Whisper hollows out people, twists machines, and rewrites memory. The Iron Synod — an old militant theocracy of sealers, archivists, and warrior-confessors — patrols the cracks and burns what crawls out. They have been losing for three hundred years. **You are a Penitent**, marked with a Synod stigma you don't remember earning, conscripted in all but name. Ashenfold Hold is where you wake up.

## Faction sketches

### The Iron Synod
A militant theocracy that survives by tradition and discipline. Members:
- **Confessors** — old, robed, archive-minded. They lead Holds.
- **Captain-Synodists** — combat field officers. Plate armour, censers on their belts. They lead patrols.
- **Scholiasts** — exiled archivists. Some leave the Synod and become hermits.
- **Penitents** — conscripts, brand-marked. Used. Trusted late if at all.

### The Choir of Wounds
The Pale Voice's followers. They believe the Whisper is a sacrament, not a sickness — that the Breach is the only honest thing the world has left. They wear black robes and carry the same kind of censer the Synod uses, lit with a different wax.

### Frontier scavengers
Pirate clans, smelter-city gangs, salvager families. Most are not aligned. A few have been corrupted. A few are run by Synod renegades (Briard).

## Tone rules for dialogue

1. **Liturgical cadence.** Synod NPCs speak in short, formal sentences. Often invoke "the Synod," "the Stigma," "the work."
2. **Brand discipline.** Never call the player by name. Always "Penitent," "marked," "the conscript."
3. **No magic words.** Use **shells**, **bolt-shots**, **censers**, **plate**, **stoles**, **lumens**, not **swords** and **wands**. (Existing equipment can stay sword-like; the *language* changes.)
4. **The Whisper is never named lightly.** NPCs hesitate before saying it. They cross themselves (a Synod gesture) or touch their stigma.
5. **No invocations of an Emperor or single deity.** The Synod prays to *the Sealing*, an abstract liturgical act, not a person.
6. **Cynicism in officers, faith in zealots, weariness in elders.** Keep the tonal three-band split.
7. **Gothic over fantasy.** Stone halls with censers, not forests with glades. Where the existing zones are pastoral (Hollow Fields), describe them as *abandoned* pastoral — *what is left*, not *what is*.
